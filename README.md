## 🔍 Dashboard Features & Insights
* **Sales Trends (Monthly & Daily):** Tracks quantity and sales fluctuations across months (January–December) and days of the week to identify peak selling periods[cite: 4].
* **City-Wise Performance (Map View):** Interactive geographical map highlighting revenue distribution across major cities[cite: 4].
* **Brand & Model Breakdown:** Compares top-performing smartphone brands (Apple, OnePlus, Samsung, Vivo, Xiaomi) and individual models by total sales[cite: 4].
* **Customer Ratings Analysis:** Funnel chart breaking down customer satisfaction scores from 1 to 5 stars[cite: 4].
* **Payment Method Distribution:** Donut chart analyzing transaction share across UPI, Debit Card, Credit Card, and Cash[cite: 4].
* **Interactive Slicers:** Dynamic filtering by **Month**, **Brand**, and **Payment Method** for granular drill-down analysis[cite: 4].
